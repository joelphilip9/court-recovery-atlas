Three ways forward

Use the right kind of work.

01 / RESETMove without proving anything.

Choose controlled mobility, light handles, form shooting, or an easy walk. The win is leaving with more confidence than you started with.

02 / REBUILDPractice the patterns.

Use gradual, coach-approved work: footwork, balance, deceleration, and low-pressure skill reps. Track how your body responds tomorrow.

03 / RETURNEarn intensity in layers.

Only add speed, contact, or volume when your clinician and training plan support it. One good session is information—not a finish line.
